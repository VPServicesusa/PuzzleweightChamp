Ways to play

Solo, side by side, or online

Single player

Climb through every world at your own pace and earn up to three stars on each level. Got three stars? Play a remix: a brand-new board every time.

Daily puzzle

One new board for everyone every day, changing at midnight Eastern time. One try, and a Game Center leaderboard.

Pass and play

2 to 6 players on one phone. Everyone plays the same board, and scores stay hidden until the last player finishes.

Online

Play friends or someone random through Game Center, best of 3, 5 or 7. Easy to Expert. Take your turn whenever you like, within 5 days.

Ranks

Win online matches to climb from Bronze to Champion, and see where you stand on the rank leaderboard.

Seasonal events

Spooky Season, Harvest, Winter Festival and more, each with its own levels and limited-time looks.

Fair and friendly

Free to play, no pressure

  • Ads only appear between puzzles, never during one, never in the daily puzzle, and never in the middle of a match.
  • Remove ads for good with a single purchase.
  • Everything in the store is for looks only. Coins are earned by playing; coin packs are optional.
  • Online chat uses preset emojis and lines only, so there's no typed chat.
  • No account to make. Your progress saves to your own iCloud and follows you to your other devices.
