PUZZLEWEIGHT CHAMP WEBSITE: VERSION 1.1 UPDATE

Upload these files the day version 1.1 is live on the App Store.

1. On github.com, open the PuzzleweightChamp repository.
2. Click Add file > Upload files.
3. Drag in these 6 files (not this text file):
     index.html, support.html, terms.html, style.css,
     shot-worlds.webp, shot-tinker.webp (new)
   Files with the same name replace the old ones.
4. Click Commit changes. The site updates in a minute or two.
   Refresh vpservicesusa.github.io/PuzzleweightChamp/ in Safari to check.

What changes: 33 puzzles and six worlds, Tinker Town (with a new
screenshot), Knot Not and Maze Rush, 6,600 levels, remixes, Expert online,
two new support answers (remixes and earning coins), and the online turn
limit corrected from 3 to 5 days.
